A civilization on the brink of collapse watches the stars

During the Cultural Revolution, a secret Chinese military project sends signals into deep space. Decades later, that decision brings humanity face to face with an alien civilization from an unstable star system: Trisolaris, ruled by the chaotic motion of three suns. What starts as a scientific mystery becomes a story about physics, strategy, and the survival of the species.

The three books in the saga

The Three-Body Problem

A physicist and an investigator uncover a video game that hides a secret: contact with a civilization doomed by its own solar system.

The Dark Forest

Humanity prepares for the Trisolaran invasion with an impossible strategy: thinking like the universe itself, where every civilization is a silent hunter.

Death's End

Cheng Xin inherits the fate of two worlds. The balance of terror that keeps the peace is more fragile than anyone imagines.
